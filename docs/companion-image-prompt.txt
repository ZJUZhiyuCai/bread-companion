Friendly and huggable, calm rather than hyperactive, slightly imperfect living presence. No backdrop, no scenery, no phone, no UI, no text, no logo, no watermark. Genuine transparent alpha background, no baked checkerboard. Full subject occupies about 80 percent of a square canvas. Camera straight-on at the face's eye height; clearly separated eye wells easy to overlay in code. Make the character visually strong enough to be the central object of a calm app.
